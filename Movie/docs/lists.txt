        G (General Audience)
        Suitable for all ages. No offensive content. (Toy Story)

        PG (Parental Guidance Suggested)
        Some material may not be suitable for children. (Harry Potter and the Sorcerer’s Stone)

        PG-13 (Parents Strongly Cautioned)
        Some material may be inappropriate for children under 13 (violence, mild language). (Most Marvel movies (Avengers, Spider-Man))

        R (Restricted)
        Under 17 requires accompanying parent or adult. Strong language, violence, nudity. (Deadpool, Logan)

        NC-17 (Adults Only)
        No one 17 and under admitted. Explicit adult content. (Very rare in mainstream, usually indie films.)
